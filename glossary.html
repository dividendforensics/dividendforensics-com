The language of REITs

A definition is
just the beginning.

Quick explanations of 25 common terms, each connected to a full guide with examples and limitations.

Stock basics

New to the vocabulary? Start with how stocks are classified and tickers, exchanges, indexes, and ETFs. These introductions are separate from the REIT and BDC lesson sequences.

25 terms

No matching terms. Try an abbreviation or a shorter word.

AFFO
An issuer-defined adjustment to FFO intended to describe recurring performance. Definitions and cash-cost exclusions differ. Explore AFFO in the full guide ↗
Cap rate
Annual property NOI divided by property value or price; a valuation input with sensitivity to both assumptions. Explore Cap rate in the full guide ↗
Common equity
The residual ownership claim after liabilities and senior claims. Common distributions are not guaranteed. Explore Common equity in the full guide ↗
Coverage
An income or cash measure divided by the distribution or expense being tested. The measure and period must match. Explore Coverage in the full guide ↗
Dilution
A reduction in a per-share claim or result as share count grows, depending on what the new capital earns. Explore Dilution in the full guide ↗
Dividend yield
Annual dividends per share divided by price. Identify whether dividends are trailing, indicated or forecast. Explore Dividend yield in the full guide ↗
DRIP
A dividend reinvestment plan that uses distributions to purchase additional shares under stated terms. Explore DRIP in the full guide ↗
Equity REIT
A REIT that generally owns or operates income-producing real estate. Explore Equity REIT in the full guide ↗
FFO
Funds from operations: a supplemental non-GAAP operating-performance measure with specified real-estate adjustments. Explore FFO in the full guide ↗
Interest coverage
A specified earnings measure divided by interest expense; definitions and cash versus accrued bases matter. Explore Interest coverage in the full guide ↗
Lease rollover
The expiration, renewal or replacement of leases, bringing opportunities to reset rents and risks of downtime. Explore Lease rollover in the full guide ↗
Mortgage REIT
A REIT that generally finances real estate through loans or mortgage-backed securities. Explore Mortgage REIT in the full guide ↗
NAV
Estimated net asset value after liabilities and other claims; sensitive to asset valuation assumptions. Explore NAV in the full guide ↗
Net lease
A lease assigning certain property expenses to the tenant as well as rent. The contract determines the allocation. Explore Net lease in the full guide ↗
NOI
Net operating income at the property level, before financing and corporate items; issuer definitions need checking. Explore NOI in the full guide ↗
Payout ratio
Distributions divided by a specified income or cash measure on a consistent basis. Explore Payout ratio in the full guide ↗
Recurring capex
Ongoing capital expenditure needed to maintain earning capacity, distinct from some growth investments. Explore Recurring capex in the full guide ↗
Refinancing
Replacing or renewing financing, potentially changing interest cost, terms, collateral or maturity. Explore Refinancing in the full guide ↗
REIT ETF
An exchange-traded fund holding REIT-related securities according to its stated strategy or index. Explore REIT ETF in the full guide ↗
Same-store NOI
NOI growth for a defined comparable property pool, which can differ between issuers and periods. Explore Same-store NOI in the full guide ↗
Straight-line rent
Accounting recognition that can spread contractual rent over a lease term and differ from cash received. Explore Straight-line rent in the full guide ↗
Tenant concentration
Dependence on a small number of tenants, measured using a disclosed base such as annualized rent. Explore Tenant concentration in the full guide ↗
Total return
The combination of income and value change over a period, with reinvestment treatment stated. Explore Total return in the full guide ↗
Triple-net lease
A net lease generally allocating property taxes, insurance and maintenance obligations to tenants, subject to contract details. Explore Triple-net lease in the full guide ↗
Weighted lease term
An average remaining lease term weighted by a disclosed base; it can conceal near-term concentrations. Explore Weighted lease term in the full guide ↗

These are concise educational explanations. See each linked guide for primary sources, scope and issuer-specific differences.

Put the terms in context

Start with the FFO reconciliation, then issuer-specific AFFO adjustments. For lending income rather than property income, use the BDC definition of NII.
