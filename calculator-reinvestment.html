The calculation

Each month: income = opening balance × annual yield ÷ 12 × (1 − withholding rate). Closing balance = opening balance + income + end-of-month contribution. Repeat for years × 12.

Hypothetical illustration

Check the arithmetic.

With a 10,000 starting balance, 6% constant annual yield, no withholding and no contributions, one modeled year ends at 10,616.78. Monthly compounding makes the result different from simply adding 600 once at year-end.

Read the limits before the result.

This is a constant-yield balance model, not a share-price or dividend-growth forecast. It assumes monthly reinvestment, fractional purchases, unchanged principal value apart from income and contributions, and no fees. Actual payment schedules, prices and taxes differ.

The output is an educational scenario. It is not a forecast, a tax determination or a personalized investment recommendation.
