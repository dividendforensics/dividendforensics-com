From the inside out, layer by layer.

What the Bureau knows about a reader, structured as concentric rings: at the centre, what is voluntary; outward, what is automatic; at the outermost layer, what the Bureau does not collect at all.

I

— Inner Ring · Voluntary —

When you subscribe to the Bureau Substack or purchase a Gumroad deep-dive, you voluntarily provide your email address (and, for paid orders, the payment information required by the platform). The Bureau receives this through the platform's standard subscriber export.

Substack and Gumroad each have their own privacy policies; the Bureau is bound by those policies for data handled within their systems.

II

— Middle Ring · Automatic —

The Bureau site is hosted as a static site. Server logs from the host include IP addresses, user-agent strings, and request timestamps, retained for standard security periods. Aggregate analytics (page views, country-level location, referrer) is collected to understand reading patterns.

No personalised tracking is applied. No third-party advertising trackers are embedded. No cookies are set beyond what the static host requires.

III

— Outer Ring · What the Bureau Does Not Collect —

The Bureau does not collect: your name, address, financial information, brokerage details, or portfolio holdings. The Bureau does not have visibility into what positions you hold, what trades you make, or what other publications you read. The Bureau cannot identify you by your reading patterns.

If, in the future, the Bureau introduces additional data collection (for example, a reader survey), it will be opt-in and the relevant data handling will be described here at that time.
