— About this room —
The Privacy room is the Bureau's reader notice — plainly stated, under one dome. The Bureau does not depend on advertising revenue, does not sell reader data, and does not run third-party tracking. This room exists to say all of that on the record. (From Latin privatus — "set apart, restricted, one's own".)
— Eight Sections · One Dome —

What is collected, and what is not.

No tracking pixels. No third-party advertising. No resale of any reader data. The Bureau's revenue is reader-funded directly, which removes the incentive that produces most privacy issues elsewhere.

I.

What is collected.

The Bureau collects only what is necessary to serve the site and respond to your communications:

  • Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, page accessed, timestamp) retained briefly for security and analytics.
  • Email address if you subscribe to the Bureau newsletter or join the Structural Income Desk™.
  • Payment information processed by the payment provider; the Bureau does not store card details.
II.

What is not collected.

The Bureau does not:

  • Run third-party advertising trackers, pixel networks, or behavioral fingerprinting.
  • Sell, lease, or share reader data with advertising platforms or data brokers.
  • Build behavioral profiles for marketing.
  • Use session replay or keystroke recording tools.
III.

Cookies & local storage.

The site uses only first-party functional cookies — for example, to remember whether you have dismissed a banner or chosen a display preference. No third-party advertising cookies are set. Your browser's "Do Not Track" signal is respected.

IV.

Email & newsletter.

If you subscribe, your email is used only to deliver Bureau dispatches. The list is not shared, sold, or rented. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any newsletter, and on unsubscribe the address is removed within 30 days.

V.

Payment processing.

Membership and product payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the external merchant-of-record payment provider. The Bureau receives only the information necessary to fulfill the order: your name, email, and the membership tier or product purchased. Card and bank details are handled exclusively by Lemon Squeezy.

VI.

Analytics & logs.

The Bureau may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as Plausible or self-hosted equivalents) that do not set tracking cookies or collect personal data. Server logs are retained briefly for security and aggregate analysis; identifying information is not extracted.

VII.

Your rights.

You may, at any time:

  • Request a copy of any personal data the Bureau holds about you.
  • Ask for that data to be corrected or deleted.
  • Withdraw consent for any non-essential processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.
VIII.

Contact & updates.

This notice may be updated as Bureau operations evolve. Material changes will be noted in the Errata with a dated entry. The data controller is 구정모 (Jeong-Mo Goo), Republic of Korea. For privacy questions, or to exercise any of the rights above, contact kjong0626@gmail.com — or via the channel listed at About.
