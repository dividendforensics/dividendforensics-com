Learning center/Beginner
Level 01 / Beginner

Build the foundation.

Start with what a REIT owns, how a shareholder gets paid, and the difference between income and investment return.

Before you start

No accounting background required. Bring curiosity and a willingness to separate a company from its share price.

What you will be able to do

Explain the business model, identify the income measure in a claim, and calculate a simple dividend return without confusing yield with safety.
