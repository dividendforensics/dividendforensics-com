Filed XXIV · V · MMXXVI · 23:14 ET
Observation

The third option-share footnote.

Reading the Q1 cov-call ETF distribution breakdown tonight. The third source — short-term gains — is once again labeled in a small footnote rather than the top-line statement. Not a violation. Not advice. But the asymmetry between how option premium and how realized gains are presented continues to widen.

Will revisit when the half-year filing arrives.

— J.M.G.

Filed XXII · V · MMXXVI · 22:40 ET
Margin

Same industry. Three calendars.

Three net-lease names, three completely different refinancing calendars in the next 24 months. The shape of the wall, not the height of the spread, is what does the work this cycle.

(Working note for the Saturday filing.)

— J.M.G.

Filed XV · V · MMXXVI · 01:08 ET
Reading

A BBB- name that did not fall off the cliff.

The BBB- Cliff™ lens is a structural one — what changes when an issuer crosses the threshold. But the threshold is also where the most patient operators do their slow work to climb back. One name in the watchlist has spent three quarters under the line and the spreads are gently narrowing again. Not yet a thesis. A note.

— J.M.G.

Filed VIII · V · MMXXVI · 00:22 ET
Marginalia

The buffer half-life of words.

The phrase "sustainable dividend" appeared in three different sell-side notes this week, applied to three names with materially different coverage ratios. The word is doing different work in each one. The Bureau habit is to label the engine; the same headline word can mean buffer-twelve-months or buffer-three-quarters depending on which page you are on.

— J.M.G.
