BDC learning room/Intermediate
BDC / Level 2

Separate earnings, cash and credit.

Rebuild NII coverage, identify noncash PIK, and compare non-accrual exposure on matching denominators.

Before you start

Complete the beginner route or understand BDC structure, market price and basic financial statements.

Four guides · Free · Self-paced. Difficulty is not a professional certification.

  1. 01

    NII and distribution coverage: match the income, denominator and period

    Reconcile regular and total common-distribution coverage without confusing reported NII, issuer-adjusted measures or cash available.

  2. 02

    PIK income: distinguish the contract, the accounting and the cash

    Understand payment-in-kind recognition, two different PIK denominators and a transparent sensitivity that does not claim to be cash earnings.

  3. 03

    Non-accruals at cost and fair value: read the exposure and the mark

    Compare matched non-accrual populations, understand why a fair-value percentage can fall without credit improving, and trace migration.

  4. 04

    BDC NAV and market discounts: separate the valuation from the trading price

    Read common NAV per share, price/NAV and capital transactions without treating a discount as a promised return.

Apply the route.

Complete each guide’s worked exercise. Retain the period, units, source and assumptions with every result. The final exercise asks you to explain what remains unknown.
