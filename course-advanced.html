Learning center/Advanced
Level 03 / Advanced

Stress the assumptions.

Move beyond a single ratio. Connect valuation, refinancing, per-share dilution and dividend funding across scenarios.

Before you start

Be able to reconcile FFO and AFFO and explain property-level NOI. Be comfortable with percentages and per-share calculations.

What you will be able to do

Build a valuation range, isolate a refinancing sensitivity and explain why company growth can differ from shareholder growth.

    NAV: an estimate with assumptions, not a price promise

    Construct a simple net asset value bridge and test how debt and capitalization rates change the result.

  2. 02

    Cap rates: reading income, price, and risk together

    Understand the cap-rate equation, differences from dividend yield, and the limits of acquisition spreads.

  3. 03

    Debt maturities: read the calendar, not just the average

    Map refinancing needs against cash, committed liquidity, lease risk, and future borrowing costs.

  4. 04

    Interest coverage: test the earnings cushion

    Understand coverage ratios, covenant definitions, and why principal repayments require a separate check.

  5. 05

    Dilution: follow your share of the business

    Distinguish a rising share count from harmful economic dilution and evaluate acquisition growth per share.

  6. 06

    Dividend cuts: analyze the funding gap before the yield

    Build a dividend stress review using recurring earnings, liquidity, debt repayments, and board disclosures.

  7. 07

    REITs and interest rates: more than one transmission channel

    Separate financing costs, property valuation, tenant demand and rent growth when thinking about interest-rate changes.

    A repeatable, evidence-first worksheet for reviewing a REIT without reducing the analysis to a single score.
