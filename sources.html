Choose the source for the question

Use Investor.gov for investor education and SEC filings for company disclosures. Nareit is an industry association and a key source for FFO definitions and sector terminology; its industry perspective is not independent investment advice. Issuer releases define company-specific measures but represent the issuer's own reporting. Read them alongside the filed financial statements.

Keep the date and definition attached

A historical filing is evidence for its reporting period. It is not a live quote or a current portfolio database. When a definition differs between companies, retain each issuer's wording and reconciliation rather than assuming the labels are comparable.
