A cabinet keeps specimens. Lamplit ecology. Four mounted entries — one for each Bureau framework, applied to its native coverage class. (From Latin cabinetum — "small chamber for displaying curiosities"; the institutional ancestor of the modern collection.)
— Four Specimens · Each Framework, Applied —

Frameworks under glass.

Each framework demonstrated on its native class. Method shown, not name called. The Bureau labels engines — not tickers.

Specimen № I
I.
— Buffer Half-Life™ · Applied —

Net Lease IG cushion-life.

Class: large-cap, investment-grade, net-lease REIT — debt paper, erosion.

Long lease maturities — twelve-plus years weighted — supply a wide buffer at issuance. The Bureau reads the compression rate of that buffer when reinvestment spreads narrow against debt costs.

A wide buffer with a fast compression rate has a shorter half-life than a narrow buffer that holds. The Bureau measures the half-life, not the width.

Class · Net Lease IGFiled · BHL™
Specimen № II
II.
— Three Clocks™ · Applied —

Self-Storage three-axis read.

Class: large-cap, investment-grade, self-storage REIT — brass chronometer.

Coverage today reads strong — short lease cycle, monthly resets. Maturity tomorrow reads concentrated — fixed-rate debt towers cluster within five-year windows. Market access reads stable — investment-grade spreads tight.

The Bureau reads all three together. The slowest clock determines durability — not the fastest.

Class · Self-Storage IGFiled · 3 Clocks™
Specimen № III
III.
— BBB- Cliff™ · Applied —

Diversified Industrial border-watch.

Class: diversified industrial REIT, lower-tier IG — stone, fracture.

A name sitting at BBB- with negative outlook reads structurally distinct from one at BBB- with stable. The Bureau watches the migration vector — leverage trend, coverage ratio drift, and refinancing window — rather than the static notch.

One notch below is not a downgrade — it is a different financing universe. The cliff is institutional.

Class · Lower IGFiled · BBB- Cliff™
Specimen № IV
IV.
— Engine Deconstruction · Applied —

Covered-Call ETF engine read.

Class: equity-linked income ETF, option-overlay structure — marble chassis.

The headline yield blends two engines: dividend stream from the underlying basket, and option premium from systematic call-writing. The Bureau separates them — durability of the dividend differs categorically from durability of the premium.

Same yield. Different engines. Different half-lives.
