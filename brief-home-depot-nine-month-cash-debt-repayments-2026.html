Home Depot’s Cash Flow Faces Debt Repayments and Acquisition Spending

By DFB editorial18 November 2026 · KST

Home Depot (NYSE: HD) generated $15.496 billion in operating cash during the nine months ended November 1, 2026, according to its November 17 earnings release. The cash-flow statement provides a different view from the quarterly sales and earnings figures released alongside it.

Subtracting $2.679 billion of capital expenditures leaves $12.817 billion, calculated by DFB. Cash dividends of $6.968 billion were below that amount, leaving a $5.849 billion difference. This is a comparison of reported cash flows, not an unrestricted cash balance or a forecast of future distributions.

Other substantial uses included $4.910 billion of long-term debt repayments and $1.813 billion of net payments for acquired businesses. These outlays are separate from capital expenditures and dividends. Debt proceeds and other cash movements also appear in the statement, so repayments alone do not measure the change in total debt.

The figures cover nine months rather than the third quarter alone. They do not establish that current cash generation will recur, and the calculated difference should not be treated as cash available for another payout. A complete cash reconciliation must include the remaining investing and financing items and exchange-rate effects.

Sources: Home Depot SEC Exhibit 99.1, November 17, 2026 · Home Depot third-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings release.
