Cisco’s Cash Returns Extend Beyond Buybacks and Dividends

By DFB editorial14 November 2026 · KST

Cisco (NASDAQ: CSCO) spent $3.651 billion on program share repurchases and dividends in its fiscal first quarter ended October 24, 2026. Its November 12 earnings release also disclosed a separate $414 million payment for shares repurchased to cover taxes when restricted stock units vested.

Operating cash flow of $4.357 billion less $502 million spent on property and equipment leaves $3.855 billion, calculated by DFB. Program buybacks and dividends were $204 million below that amount. Adding the separate equity-withholding outlay brings the combined payments to $4.065 billion, or $210 million above cash generated after this capital spending.

The distinction matters because the withholding payment is reported separately from the company’s stock-repurchase program. It should not be silently combined with program buybacks or treated as an additional dividend. The figures are quarterly cash movements, not earnings or future repurchase commitments.

This comparison is not the change in Cisco’s cash balance. Debt transactions, acquisitions and investment flows also affect cash. One quarter does not establish whether these outlays will recur or determine the sustainability of future distributions.

Sources: Cisco Form 8-K, November 12, 2026 · SEC Exhibit 99.1: Cisco fiscal 2027 first-quarter results · Cisco investor-relations earnings release (PDF).
