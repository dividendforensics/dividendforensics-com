Founding Membership

The article ends. The record continues.

Dividend Forensics Bureau is preparing one founding membership for readers who want ongoing, filing-anchored coverage of what changes behind the payout after a public article is published.

Membership is not open for payment. Pricing and an opening date will be published only after the initial issuer records, delivery workflow, and member access are ready.

What is being built

A live research record, not a second article feed

  • A weekly Desk Note focused on material changes across covered issuers
  • Filing-driven Issuer Record updates after material disclosures are reviewed
  • Source-linked payout, earnings, leverage, and capital-structure calculations
  • Debt maturity, refinancing, and capital-access change logs
  • A periodic deep dive that connects the current filing to the existing record
  • Change history, unresolved items, and the next facts that would alter the analysis
Founding structure

One membership. One research standard.

The Bureau is not opening with a maze of General, Premium, and Founding tiers. The initial paid offering is being designed as one membership with the same research access for every individual member.

Pricing is intentionally not posted yet. The product will open only when the promised records and delivery path can be shown, tested, and maintained without lowering the standard of the public work.

Free vs. membership

Public research explains what happened.
Membership is being built to preserve what changed next.

Coverage discipline

Start narrow. Maintain the record.

Initial coverage will be deliberately limited and named before enrollment opens. DFB will not advertise a large universe before the underlying issuer records exist.

Each record is intended to connect reported earnings measures, payout mechanics, debt and refinancing activity, capital access, share-count changes, and unresolved disclosure questions. Coverage expands only when that record can be maintained.

Individual membership

Organizational use is a separate conversation

The founding membership is being designed for individual research access. Family offices, investment firms, wealth managers, media organizations, and financial platforms that need internal organizational use, redistribution rights, data delivery, or bespoke verification should use the Research Desk instead.

Method

The standard does not change behind a paywall

Every material figure should trace to a company disclosure. Company-reported measures stay separate from DFB calculations. Announced, priced, issued, settled, outstanding, and repaid are not treated as interchangeable states. Unknowns remain unknown until the record changes.

Then test the payout.
