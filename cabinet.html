— About this room —
The Cabinet is the Bureau's curated catalog. Where the Periodicals Room shows the latest dispatch on the open shelf, the Cabinet holds the longer, source-cited deep dives — single-ticker readings, sector overviews, and framework methods. These are full readings, not summaries, and require a small reader fee. (From French cabinet — "the chamber where specimens are displayed".)
