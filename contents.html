— About this room —
The Contents is the Bureau's site directory — the cylinder rotunda from which every other room can be entered. Sixteen rooms in three wings: the Imperial Lock (Rooms I–VI, the canonical entries), the Doctrinal Wing (VII–XI, the working annexes), and the Front-Matter (XII–XVI, the standards). (From Latin contenta — "that which is contained".)
