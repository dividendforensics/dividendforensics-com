Analog Devices Returned Nearly All of Its Fiscal 2026 Free Cash Flow

By DFB editorial25 November 2026 · KST

Analog Devices (NASDAQ: ADI) returned about $5.41 billion in cash dividends and share repurchases in its fiscal year ended October 31, 2026. That was approximately 99% of its $5.45 billion in free cash flow, based on the cash-flow statement furnished with the chipmaker’s November 24 Form 8-K.

Analog Devices defines its non-GAAP free cash flow as operating cash flow less net additions to property, plant and equipment. On that basis, free cash flow rose about 27% from fiscal 2025. But repurchases increased faster than cash dividends and accounted for most of the increase in cash returned to shareholders. The combined figure includes repurchases already made, not unused buyback authorization.

That comparison leaves out other investing and financing flows. The company separately reported about $1.54 billion in acquisition payments, net of cash acquired. The 99% figure therefore describes how shareholder payouts compared with free cash flow over the year. It does not show that a particular payout was funded with debt. Whether buybacks continue at that pace remains an open question.

Sources: SEC Form 8-K, Exhibit 99.1 · Analog Devices fiscal 2026 results.
