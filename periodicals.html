The Saturday Letter.

One reading, filed weekly.

The Bureau's weekly correspondence. A single covered name read through one signal framework — Buffer Half-Life™, Three Clocks™, or BBB- Cliff™ — closed with a structural assessment.

Filed to Substack. Open to all readers. Free.

Quarterly · End of March / June / September / December

The Quarterly Doctrine.

A framework review, filed quarterly.

A doctrinal review of the Bureau's three signal frameworks. Calibration drift, threshold refinements, and any structural reconsiderations are filed here — together with new Concordance terms added during the quarter.

Open to all readers. Filed to the Archive.

Annual · December · Anno Closing

The Charter Review.

The Bureau, read against itself.

Once a year, the Bureau reads its own Charter against the year filed. Where the doctrine held; where it bent; where it was amended at the Errata desk. The annual self-audit, on the record.

Open to all readers. Filed to the Charter.
