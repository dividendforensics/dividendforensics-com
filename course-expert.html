Your learning route.

  1. 01

    Expert workshop: normalize AFFO across competing definitions

    Rebuild two hypothetical issuer reconciliations, distinguish owner earnings from cash timing, and test whether apparent dividend coverage survives normalization.

  2. 02

    Expert workshop: build NAV through joint ventures

    Reconcile proportionate ownership with consolidated reporting, eliminate duplicate assets, and stress a multi-entity NAV model.

  3. 03

    Expert workshop: model refinancing, fees, and dilution

    Build partial-year and full-year earnings bridges for debt refinancing with and without equity, then reconcile the separate cash budget.

  4. 04

    Expert workshop: write a falsifiable REIT research memo

    Separate facts from assumptions, test opposing explanations, and turn a valuation spreadsheet into an auditable research document.

Apply what you have read

The practical exercise.

Prepare a two-page research memo and a supporting calculation sheet. Include one operating scenario, one financing scenario, a counterargument and the next disclosure that could change your conclusion. Use the workshop self-checks to test consistency.
