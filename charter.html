— The Founding Doctrine · Six Articles —

The position, in plain words.

Six articles. One sentence at the head; commentary beneath. The full position is short on purpose — anything that does not fit here does not belong in the charter.

I.

Diagnostics, not advice.

The Bureau publishes structural reads. We do not name personal positions, we do not recommend transactions, we do not address individual circumstances.

Action verbs — buy, sell, hold — do not appear in our copy. Diagnostic verbs — improving, stable, pressured — do.

II.

Structural, not surface.

The same headline yield can be paid from four very different sources: AFFO, NII, option premium, return of capital. The Bureau separates them.

What the screen shows as "yield" is, structurally, four different promises. We label which is which.

III.

Frameworks, not forecasts.

Buffer Half-Life™, Three Clocks™, BBB- Cliff™ — three lenses through which the same dividend looks structurally different.

We do not predict. We measure how much cushion is paying for the distribution, how soon the refinancing window opens, and what happens at the credit cliff.

IV.

Independent, from Seoul.

No issuer pays for coverage. No platform pays for placement. The Bureau is funded by readers — through Substack subscriptions and Gumroad deep-dive sales.

The editor is based in Seoul. The coverage is U.S. income assets. The lens is structural.

V.

Closed positions disclosed.

The editor holds no positions in the names covered. Where this changes — even briefly — disclosure is filed before the dispatch.

VI.

Corrections preserved.

The original is not deleted. The correction is filed alongside. The Errata Desk is open.
