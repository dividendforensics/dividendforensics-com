A charter is signed once. It stands. The Bureau's founding doctrine, in six articles. Read once; binding thereafter. (From Latin carta — "paper, formal grant"; the document on which an institution is founded.)
— Six Articles · Declarative Chamber —

Founding doctrine, in six.

Position, Method, Universe, Cadence, Independence, the Reader. Each article stands; together, they bind.

Art. № I
I.
— Article I · Position —

The Bureau is independent.

Reader-funded. Sell-side detached. No issuer, platform, or asset manager pays the Bureau. No sponsored content. No affiliate revenue on covered names. No compensation tied to ratings.

Independent by construction — not aspiration.

Art. № II
II.
— Article II · Method —

Diagnostic, not advisory.

The Bureau reads engines, not yields. Three signal frameworks govern every dispatch:

Buffer Half-Life™ — the time-axis of the cushion. Three Clocks™ — coverage, maturity, market access. BBB- Cliff™ — the credit migration threshold.

Diagnostic language only. No buy / sell / hold.

Art. № III
III.
— Article III · Universe —

Locked coverage. May MMXXVI.

Core Coverage (40%) — large-cap IG REITs, BDCs.
Satellite Income (25%) — cov-call ETFs, MLPs.
Structured Yield (25%) — CLO CEFs, preferreds.
Adjacent Territory (10%) — utilities, global infra.

Excluded Territory: small-cap, mREITs, distressed.

Art. № IV
IV.
— Article IV · Cadence —

Fixed schedule. No off-cycle alerts.

Monday, Wednesday, Friday — dispatches to the Archive. Saturday — the Substack letter. No emergency notes. No market-hour bulletins.

The Bureau publishes by the clock, not by the headline.

Art. № V
V.
— Article V · Independence —

The position is independent because the money is.

No advertising on Bureau pages. No sponsored research. No paid promotional placements in dispatches. Reader funding only — through the Vault's Structural Income Desk™.

One source of revenue. One direction of accountability.

Art. № VI
VI.
— Article VI · The Reader —

The Bureau serves the reader.

Not the issuer. Not the platform. Not the broker. No personal advice. No portfolio recommendations. No predictions of price.

Structural diagnostics, filed openly, read at the reader's own discretion. This is not a prediction — structural assessment.
