BDC learning room/Beginner
BDC / Level 1

Understand the lending business.

Explain what a BDC owns, distinguish trading liquidity from borrower liquidity, and find the relevant filing.

Before you start

No accounting background required. Start with the difference between a fund share and a loan to a borrower.

Four guides · Free · Self-paced. Difficulty is not a professional certification.

  1. 01

    What is a BDC? Follow the fund, the borrowers and the shareholder

    Understand the closed-end structure, the difference between borrower interest and shareholder distributions, and why a traded share is not a redeemable loan.

  2. 02

    From origination to repayment: how a BDC investment earns and loses money

    Trace a loan through commitment, funding, recognized income, amendments, valuation and exit without confusing commitments with invested assets.

  3. 03

    Internal and external BDC management: follow the expense contract

    Compare management arrangements by what shareholders actually bear, including asset-based fees, incentive compensation, reimbursements and waivers.

  4. 04

    Read a BDC filing in order: four statements and the investment schedule

    Build a source map for portfolio assets, recognized income, distributions and cash before collecting ratios or copying a presentation.

Apply the route.

Complete each guide’s worked exercise. Retain the period, units, source and assumptions with every result. The final exercise asks you to explain what remains unknown.
