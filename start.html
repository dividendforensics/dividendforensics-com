— Five Minutes · Four Steps —

A short tour before the rooms.

The Bureau is a museum of structural diagnostics on dividend-paying U.S. issuers — sixteen rooms, each with a single purpose. This page is the welcome mat. Read these four steps, then the rooms make sense.

Step 1 · What the Bureau is

Diagnostics, not advice.

The Dividend Forensics Bureau is an independent research boutique publishing structural diagnostics on U.S.-listed dividend-paying issuers. It is operated from Seoul by a single editor, Jeong-Mo Goo, and funded directly by readers — through the Structural Income Desk™ membership and open-access Gumroad guides, with a free weekly letter on Substack.

The Bureau does not recommend transactions. The Bureau does not address individual circumstances. The Bureau does not use action verbs — no "buy", no "sell", no "hold". The Bureau uses diagnostic verbs — improving, stable, pressured, compressing, migrating.

The full editorial position is in the Charter; the legal frame is in the Disclaimer.

"Yield is observed. Structure is examined.
The buffer is what is paying it."

— Bureau standing line · Charter

Step 3 · Three frameworks worth remembering

Three lenses, one yield.

The Bureau publishes through three analytical frameworks. They are lenses, not predictive instruments. They do not forecast prices. They read the structure underneath a headline distribution.

Buffer Half-Life™
How long the cushion paying the dividend would last if cash flow fell here, today.
Three Clocks™
Coverage today · Maturity tomorrow · Market access when tomorrow arrives — layered time horizons.
BBB- Cliff™
The non-linear drop in capital access when a credit rating crosses from investment-grade into high-yield.

Definitions of every recurring term sit in the Concordance. Where each framework appears in the corpus is filed in the Cabinet.
