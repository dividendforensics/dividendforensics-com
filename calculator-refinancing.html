The calculation

Annual interest change = refinanced principal × (new rate − old rate). Per-share expense change = annual interest change ÷ common shares. Positive means more interest expense; negative means savings.

Hypothetical illustration

Check the arithmetic.

Refinancing 200 million from 3% to 5% adds 4 million in annual interest. Across 100 million common shares, the isolated annual interest increase is 0.04 per share.

Read the limits before the result.

Assumes unchanged principal for a full year. Excludes issuance fees, amortization, hedges, taxes, capitalized interest, partial-year timing and changes in share count. The result is an expense sensitivity, not an FFO or AFFO forecast.

The output is an educational scenario. It is not a forecast, a tax determination or a personalized investment recommendation.
