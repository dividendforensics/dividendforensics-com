Your assignment: test a cash-distribution claim.

A draft note says, “Apple produced more cash than earnings, so its cash after capital expenditure was available for dividends and buybacks.” Locate the missing steps before accepting that conclusion. An operating company has cash tied to customers, suppliers, inventory, investment and financing. A profitable year and a positive cash-flow measure answer only part of the funding question.

  1. Rebuild operating cash flow from net income and every signed adjustment.
  2. Define cash capex and calculate a simple free-cash-flow measure.
  3. Deduct actual cash dividends and repurchases, preserving their cash-paid basis.
  4. Reconcile the full change in cash and distinguish repurchase dollars from changes in shares.

Use the FY2024 column of Apple FY2024 10-K ↗, particularly pages 32–33 and Note 10. All cash-flow inputs below are USD millions. Negative values are cash uses or negative reconciliation adjustments. The answers open with native disclosure controls and remain readable without JavaScript.

A negative result here says those two cash uses exceeded this defined annual FCF amount; it is not the ending cash balance or a finding of insolvency.

Reveal the two explicitly labeled payout ratios

Cash dividends and equivalents ÷ defined FCF = 15,234 ÷ 108,807 × 100 = 14.0009%. Cash dividends plus repurchases ÷ defined FCF = 110,183 ÷ 108,807 × 100 = 101.2646%. These annual arithmetic comparisons have different numerators. Neither establishes future dividend capacity or an appropriate buyback price.

The 11,688 share-compensation expense was added back in the operating-cash bridge. That does not make compensation economically free: the employee receives value, and shares issued can offset part of the repurchase effect. The Note 10 table measures beginning and ending shares outstanding. It is not the weighted-average diluted share denominator used for earnings per share. Dividing profit by ending shares does not reproduce reported diluted EPS.

Positive FCF is not cash available for distribution.

This lab’s FCF is a defined non-GAAP calculation, not a separately reported GAAP subtotal, a company forecast or a universal issuer definition. Its PP&E deduction does not identify maintenance versus growth spending. It also excludes other investing and financing transactions, future commitments and changes in the cash required to run the business. Compare definitions before comparing companies.

Check the conclusion before using the number.

1. Why not use net income plus depreciation as operating cash flow?

That shortcut omits share compensation, the other reconciliation adjustment and all six operating-asset and liability changes. The omitted items have different signs. Use the complete bridge instead of treating one add-back as the entire reconciliation.

2. Does the −1,376 allocation residual prove a dividend shortfall?

No. It combines dividends and discretionary repurchases under the stated FCF definition. Dividends alone are a different numerator, and the residual also omits other investing and financing cash flows. It cannot establish a future payment decision or liquidity failure.

3. Can I subtract dividends, buybacks and net financing from FCF?

That double-counts dividends and buybacks because net financing already includes both. Reconcile with full operating, investing and financing totals, or explicitly remove already-counted items before adding the remaining categories.

4. Does a zero FX worksheet input mean the company has no currency exposure?

No. This source presents no separate exchange-rate-effects line in its cash statement. Zero is a worksheet convention to reproduce this particular statement, whose reported categories already tie. It is not evidence of zero foreign-currency business exposure.

5. Rewrite the opening claim in a supportable way.

Apple’s FY2024 statement reports 118,254 of operating cash flow. Deducting 9,447 of PP&E cash purchases gives 108,807 under this lab’s FCF definition. Cash dividends and repurchases total 110,183; the resulting −1,376 residual is one subtotal within a complete cash statement whose cash change is −794. Availability for future distributions remains a separate question. All amounts are USD millions.
