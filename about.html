About the Bureau

The Bureau studies structure — not portfolios.

The Dividend Forensics Bureau is an independent boutique research operation analyzing the structural durability of dividends across U.S. income assets. Founded in MMXXIV, the Bureau publishes generalized structural research using proprietary frameworks — Buffer Half-Life™, Three Clocks™, BBB- Cliff™ — across a network of channels including dispatches, archives, and reading chambers.

The Bureau is edited by Jeong-Mo Goo (구정모) from Seoul, Republic of Korea. The work is published in English; correspondence is conducted in English.

The yield is what you are paid.
The buffer is what is paying it.

What the Bureau Does

The Bureau reads the structure beneath the payment — the coverage that funds the dividend, the maturity wall that lies further out, the refinancing that has not yet been done, the spread that has not yet widened.

It does not predict. It does not recommend. It does not rank. It notes, in small careful entries, how much of the yield is owed to the present and how much is owed to a future that has not yet arrived.

Where to Read

Dispatches are filed on a tri-weekly cadence. Structural research is bound into the Archive. The doctrinal annex is filed in the Reading Chambers. The editorial covenant is set down in the Charter.
