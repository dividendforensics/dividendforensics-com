Independent researcher, writing from Seoul.

Jeong-Mo Goo is an independent researcher focused on dividend durability and refinancing risk across REITs and income-oriented equities. The Dividend Forensics Bureau is operated as a one-editor research desk — a deliberate scale chosen to keep the position independent.

Author of the Bureau's three signal frameworks: Buffer Half-Life™, Three Clocks™, and BBB- Cliff™. Each is the Bureau's intellectual property; each is documented in the Concordance and applied across the Archive.

The Bureau publishes on a fixed cadence (Monday / Wednesday / Friday for dispatches, Saturday for the Substack letter) and maintains a locked coverage universe. Membership opens MMXXVI as the Structural Income Desk™, restricted under the Vault.

I.

The Position.

Independent · reader-funded · sell-side detached.

The Bureau accepts no payment from issuers, platforms, or asset managers. No sponsored content, no affiliate revenue on the covered names, no compensation tied to ratings.

The position is independent because the money is.

II.

The Method.

Diagnostic, not advisory. The Bureau reads engines, not yields:

  • Buffer Half-Life™ — the time-axis of the cushion.
  • Three Clocks™ — coverage / maturity / market access.
  • BBB- Cliff™ — the migration threshold.

Diagnostic language only — never buy/sell/hold.

III.

The Universe.

Locked coverage, locked May MMXXVI:

  • Tier 1 (40%) — large-cap IG REITs, BDCs.
  • Tier 2 (25%) — cov-call ETFs · MLPs.
  • Tier 2 (25%) — CLO CEFs · preferreds.
  • Tier 2 (10%) — utilities · global infra.

Small-cap, mREITs, distressed — excluded.
