Jeong-Mo Goo is an independent researcher focused on dividend durability and refinancing risk across REITs and income-oriented equities.

Author of the Buffer Half-Life™, Three Clocks™, and BBB- Cliff™ analytical frameworks — three lenses through which the same headline yield reads structurally different.

The Bureau operates from Seoul as a single-editor research boutique. No issuer pays for coverage, no platform pays for placement. Readers fund the dispatches; the membership funds the deep work.

Coverage scope: U.S.-listed dividend-paying issuers, organised in two tiers (below). Outside that scope, the Bureau does not opine.
