Bureau Correction Policy

Substantive errors — figures, attributions, framework applications — are corrected within seven days of detection and entered in this Register, dated and signed.

Typographical errors are corrected silently in place; no register entry follows.

Methodological revisions — refinements to the diagnostic frameworks themselves — appear in the Concordance under Method Evolution, where each revision is dated and traced.

To report an error, write to errata@dividendforensics.com.

Forensic Integrity

Each Register state is sealed with a SHA‑256 fingerprint of its register identifier, volume, date, and current entry status. The fingerprint is computed at issuance and re-verified upon each substantive amendment; it confirms that no entry has been silently altered between sealings.
