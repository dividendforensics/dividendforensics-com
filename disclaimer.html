— Editorial Position · Three Tiers —

Read it once. It stands.

The Bureau publishes structural diagnostics on dividend durability and refinancing risk. It is not investment advice, a recommendation, or a solicitation. Three tiers cover what the Bureau is not, what attaches to every working text, and what the reader acknowledges by reading.

A
Editorial Position

What the Bureau is — and is not.

The Dividend Forensics Bureau is an independent research operation publishing structural diagnostics on dividend durability and refinancing risk across U.S. income assets.

The Bureau is not a registered investment advisor, a broker-dealer, a regulated financial institution, or a fiduciary. The editor, Jeong-Mo Goo, is an independent researcher operating from Seoul, Republic of Korea.

Bureau dispatches are diagnostic readings, not advice. Diagnostic verbs are permitted (improving, stable, pressured); action verbs (buy, sell, hold, trim, add) do not appear in any Bureau text.

No issuer pays for coverage. No platform pays for placement. The Bureau is funded by reader subscriptions (Substack) and a small catalog of deep-dive reports (Gumroad). Editorial choices are independent of any commercial relationship.

B
Full Mode · Attaches to Every Reading

The standing notice under every full-mode dispatch.

This notice attaches by reference to every Bureau full-mode essay, deep dive, and structural reading published on the Bureau site, Substack, Medium, GuruFocus, Benzinga, or any other channel under the Dividend Forensics Bureau or Jeong-Mo Goo byline.

  • This is a structural diagnostic, not investment advice, a recommendation, or a solicitation to buy, sell, hold, or refrain from any security.
  • The author holds no position in the securities discussed unless explicitly disclosed. Where the author holds a position, the disclosure is filed in the dispatch header before the body.
  • The Bureau makes no guarantee of completeness, timeliness, or freedom from error. Best efforts are made; sources are cited where used.
  • Past distribution patterns, credit history, or operating results do not predict future outcomes. Structural reads identify pressure points; they do not forecast prices or distributions.
  • The reader is solely responsible for any decision made after reading. Bureau dispatches are not a substitute for the reader's own analysis, consultation with a licensed advisor, or independent due diligence.
  • This is not a prediction — structural assessment.
C
Reader Acknowledgment · Frame

What the reader acknowledges by entering.

By reading any Bureau dispatch — on the Bureau site, on Substack, Medium, GuruFocus, Benzinga, or any republication — the reader acknowledges the following:

  • The reader is not seeking personal investment advice from the Bureau editor.
  • The reader will form their own conclusions from the diagnostic material and any other inputs they consider relevant.
  • The reader retains full responsibility for any action they take or do not take after reading.
  • The reader has not relied on Bureau publications as a substitute for their own analysis, professional advice, or independent due diligence.
  • Disagreement with a Bureau diagnostic is welcomed; conversion of a diagnostic into a personal recommendation is the reader's own act.

The Bureau is filed under doctrine — not advice. Read accordingly.
