— Editorial Disclaimer · Three Tiers —

Take notice before you read further.

Tier A is the universal notice that applies to every Bureau publication. Tier B applies to full-mode analytical essays. Tier C applies to membership-only material. Read the tier that matches what you are reading.

— Tier A · Universal —

For every Bureau publication, including this site.

The Dividend Forensics Bureau publishes independent research and structural diagnostics on U.S.-listed dividend-paying issuers. The Bureau is operated by Jeong-Mo Goo from Seoul.

Nothing on this site, or in any Bureau publication, constitutes investment advice, a recommendation to buy or sell any security, or any form of personalised financial advice. The Bureau does not know your financial situation, your investment objectives, your tax position, your jurisdiction, or your risk tolerance. The publications are general informational reads, written for an audience of professional and informed readers.

Securities mentioned in Bureau publications may experience significant price volatility, distribution changes, or total loss of value. Past performance is not indicative of future results. The editor's analytical frameworks are interpretive lenses, not predictive instruments.

The editor holds no positions in the names covered, except where disclosed at the head of a specific dispatch.

— Tier B · Full-Mode Essays —

For long-form analytical dispatches.

Analytical essays — including those applying the Buffer Half-Life™, Three Clocks™, or BBB- Cliff™ frameworks — are structural readings of public information. They are not forecasts. They are not price targets. They do not address any reader's individual circumstances.

Where a dispatch references issuer financial data, the data is drawn from publicly filed disclosures (SEC filings, issuer investor-relations materials, press releases). The Bureau does not have access to material non-public information. Data interpretation is the editor's own.

If a dispatch contains an error of fact or interpretation, the correction is filed at errata.html. Originals are preserved with strikethrough; corrections are visible alongside.

— Tier C · Membership Material —

For Structural Income Desk™ subscribers.

Structural Income Desk™ membership material includes pre-publication previews, structural alerts, and the quarterly Structural Income Outlook. All Tier A and Tier B provisions apply equally.

Membership material is for the named subscriber only. Forwarding, republication, or redistribution without written permission is not permitted.

The Bureau is not registered as an investment adviser. The Bureau does not provide personalised investment advice as part of any membership offering. Membership material is informational research, intended for the reader's own analytical use.
