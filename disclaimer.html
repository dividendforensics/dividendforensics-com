Editorial Notices.

— Climate · Mid Wing —
A notice draws a boundary. Transitional ecology. Six notices stand at the threshold of every Bureau dispatch — neither chamber nor reading room; a passage between. (From Latin nota — "mark, sign"; the boundary mark.)
— Six Notices · At the Threshold —

Six notices. Filed at the threshold.

Each notice marks a boundary the Bureau holds — what is filed; what is not; what the reader retains.

Notice № N-01
I.
— Notice I · Not Investment Advice —

The Bureau is not a registered investment advisor.

No content on this site constitutes investment advice. The Bureau is an independent research publication; it is not registered as an investment advisor with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority, or any equivalent authority.

Nothing filed here is offered as a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security.

Notice № N-02
II.
— Notice II · Diagnostic Only —

Diagnostic, not advisory.

The Bureau publishes diagnostic readings of dividend durability and refinancing structure. Language is descriptive — improving, stable, pressured, compressing — not prescriptive.

The Bureau does not issue action verbs: buy, sell, hold, add, trim, exit appear nowhere in its filings.

Notice № N-03
III.
— Notice III · No Personalization —

Filings are general, not personal.

All Bureau filings are addressed to a general readership. They are not tailored to any individual's financial situation, investment objectives, risk tolerance, tax position, or time horizon.

No content here should be construed as personalized advice. Readers seeking personalized advice should consult a licensed professional.

Notice № N-04
IV.
— Notice IV · Publisher Exception —

The Bureau operates as a bona fide publisher.

The Bureau operates under the publisher exception to investment-advisor registration: impersonal in content, bona fide and disinterested in posture, and of regular circulation by fixed cadence.

The Bureau holds no position in any covered name. No issuer, platform, or asset manager pays the Bureau. No compensation is tied to ratings or coverage.

Notice № N-05
V.
— Notice V · Forward-Looking Statements —

No predictions. Structural assessment only.

Bureau filings include no forecasts of price, total return, or future performance. Closing phrase across all dispatches: "This is not a prediction — structural assessment."

Where the Bureau reads engines, buffers, or refinancing structure, the reading is of present structure — not of future market outcomes.

Notice № N-06
VI.
— Notice VI · Reader's Discretion —

The discretion remains the reader's.

Any decision to buy, sell, hold, or refrain from acting on any security rests entirely with the reader. The Bureau filed a reading; the reader brings the decision.

The Bureau accepts no liability for any loss, direct or consequential, arising from any action taken on the basis of its filings.

— Acknowledgment —
By reading this site, the reader acknowledges: the Bureau publishes diagnostic research, not advice; the Bureau holds no position in covered names; and the discretion remains the reader's.
